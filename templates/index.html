Core Features

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)

Detects prolonged eye closure using the geometric ratio of 6 eye landmarks per eye. Threshold breach over 20 frames triggers an alert.

Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR)

Identifies yawning episodes by measuring vertical mouth opening against horizontal span across 20 mouth landmarks.

Audio Alert System

Tiered audio notifications escalate with repeated drowsiness detections, providing gentle-then-urgent warnings.

Live Metric Chart

Real-time rolling chart of EAR and MAR values with visual threshold markers — see exactly how close to the limit you are.

How It Works

1

Grant Camera Access

Your browser requests webcam permission. Video never leaves your device — frames are processed locally through the Flask backend running on localhost.

2

Landmark Detection

Every 100 ms, a JPEG frame is sent to the backend where MediaPipe FaceMesh identifies 468 3-D facial landmarks in real time.

3

Geometric Analysis

EAR and MAR are computed from landmark coordinates. Values are returned to the browser, charted, and checked against thresholds.

4

Alert if Drowsy

If EAR falls below 0.25 for 20+ consecutive frames, or MAR exceeds 0.75 for 20 of 30 frames (yawn), an audio alert fires immediately.
